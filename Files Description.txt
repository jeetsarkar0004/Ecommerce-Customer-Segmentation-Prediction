Files Description:

data.csv is the raw dataset.
cleaned_data.csv is the data after cleaning, it is required for PowerBI Dashboard.
rmf.csv is the segmentation data, it is required for PowerBI Dashbaord. 
model_comparison.csv is the data with all the model names and their metric scores.
Segmentation and Prediction.jpynb is the python code.
Customer_Segmentation_Capstone.pptx is the PowerPoint Presentation file.
Dashboard.pbix is the PowerBI report.
